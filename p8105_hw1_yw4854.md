# Problem 1

The dataset contains data on adult penguins across 3 species in the
Palmer Archipelago, Antarctica. The dataset has 344 rows and 8 columns.

Important variables：

- `Species`: Penguin species (Adelie, Chinstrap, Gentoo)

- `Island`: Penguin Habitat (Biscoe, Dream, Torgersen)

- `bill_length_mm`: bill length (millimeters)

- `bill_depth_mm`: bill depth (millimeters)

- `flipper_length_mm`: flipper length (millimeters)

- `body_mass_g`: body mass (grams)

- `sex`: penguin sex (female, male)

- `year`: study year

- mean of norm_samp works.Because it is numeric.
- mean of norm_greater_0 works. Because it is logical.
- mean of char_vec doesn’t work. Because is not numeric or logical.
- mean of factor_vec doesn’t work. Because is not numeric or logical.

### convert variables from one type to another

``` r
as.numeric(pull(hw1_df, norm_greater_0))
as.numeric(pull(hw1_df, char_vec))
as.numeric(pull(hw1_df, factor_vec))
```

- norm_greater_0 convert to 1 and 0. This can explain why mean()
  succeeded. Because 0 and 1 are number. For example 0.4 means there are
  4 variable greater than 0.

- char_vec convert to NA, because words cannot be parsed into numbers.

- factor_vec convert to 1,2 and 3. Because 1,2 and 3 are categorical
  labels, they have no meaning in terms of the mean.
